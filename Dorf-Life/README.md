# Dorf Life, a mod for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead

This mod focuses on adding caverns that randomly breach the depths of subway tunnels (if only sewers were an option, but they won't mesh up cleanly), expanding the underground with areas full of various flora, fauna, and strange encounters.

This version targets **Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead 0.I "Ito" (0.I-1)**. Place the included Dorf_Life folder in your data/mods folder. If updating the mod, it is recommended to delete the older version of the folder rather than just dumping the new one onto the old one.

## Features

* Caverns of various sizes and randomized depths, with various debris, pillars, and other things to make the appearance more organic.
* Pools (including magma, of course), pockets of radiation, evidence of failed expeditions, buried ruins (mundane or otherwise), and stranger things can show up.
* Exotic underground "plants" that can be harvested at various periods of the year, with varied uses as well.
* A wide variety of cavern critters that can show up, at a fairly consistent spawn rate.
* Unique sprites for all terrain added (with a focus on still being visually distinct in the dark), and for almost all items added.
* Finales at the bottom of every cavern that reaches maximum depth, including the largest ones: a long-abandoned dwarven hall, sealed around something its builders never should have dug up.
* Surface caves in the woods now open into multi-level caverns using the same system, with rarer deep caves that go all the way down to a finale.
* A "Strike the Earth!" scenario that starts you deep in the caverns beside your expedition's supply wagon, plus a Spelunker profession.
* Cave farming: scrape spores off of cavern plants and sow them into beds of cave moss to grow your own, no sunlight required.

## Notes

* The deep hall's vault is sealed with a hidden stone door.  Look for a lever.  Whatever you do, don't mine out the glittering spire inside.  (You're going to mine out the spire, aren't you?)
* To farm, build a cave moss bed (Construction, farming tab) on cavern moss, or on rock or dirt floor if you bring withered plants or plant fibre and some water.  Then sow spores into it.  Sown beds grow into a harvestable patch at the turn of the season, and harvested patches regrow every season after that.
* This mod overrides the vanilla "Cave" and "Cave w/ trail" overmap specials.  The vanilla "Rat Cave" is left as-is.
